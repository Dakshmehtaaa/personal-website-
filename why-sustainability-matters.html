The business case

Where the advantage actually comes from

Risk & Compliance

CSRD, the EU Taxonomy and supply-chain due-diligence law are moving from voluntary to mandatory. A strategy already in motion means less scramble when the deadline lands.

Lower Operating Costs

Measuring emissions and resource use surfaces waste — energy, materials, logistics — that was invisible before. What gets measured usually gets managed down.

Access to Capital

Lenders and investors increasingly screen ESG performance before price. A credible rating — CDP, EcoVadis — opens financing that simply isn't there without one.

Talent Attraction

Sustainability commitments show up in where people choose to work. It's something candidates actively screen for, especially early- and mid-career hires.

Market Access

Large clients increasingly require ESG data before they'll sign. RFIs, tenders and supplier questionnaires now carry real commercial weight, not just paperwork.

Brand & Trust

Customers and partners notice the gap between claims and evidence. A real strategy is what lets a company make claims it can actually defend.

Getting started

Free resources to get started

No budget needed for the first step. These are free, and where I'd point a company starting today.

GHG Protocol The global standard for measuring Scope 1, 2 & 3 emissions — the starting point for any carbon inventory. Science Based Targets initiative Free guidance and tools for setting emissions targets that hold up to scrutiny. CDP — How to Disclose How to disclose environmental data for the first time, on a platform used by thousands of companies. GRI Standards The world's most-used sustainability reporting standards, free to download in full. UN UN Global Compact Free to join — sustainability principles and practical guidance for businesses of any size. EFRAG — ESRS Standards The official CSRD reporting standards, published in full and free to read. EU Taxonomy Compass A free tool to check which activities count as environmentally sustainable under EU law. B Impact Assessment A free, confidential self-assessment of your company's impact on workers, community and environment.

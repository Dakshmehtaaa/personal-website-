01 /THE BUSINESS CASE

Good for business.Grounded in action.

Six reasons to act. Start with the priorities that matter to your organisation.

01

Manage risk

Know which reporting requirements apply to your business. Keep the supporting evidence organised.

02

Find avoidable costs

Track energy, materials and waste to see where resources—and budget—are being lost.

03

Support financing decisions

Give lenders and investors clearer information on your performance, exposure and plans.

04

Engage your people

Turn commitments into projects people can contribute to, with clear ownership and visible progress.

05

Answer customer expectations

Respond to tenders and supplier questionnaires with consistent, traceable ESG data.

06

Build credible trust

Back up sustainability claims with evidence. Make it easy to see what has changed.

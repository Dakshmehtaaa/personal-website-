Read the script
  1. For most companies, sustainability reporting used to be optional. Now it’s inevitable.
  2. Yes, it’s about protecting the planet and people. But there’s a part many companies miss.
  3. Your customers are comparing. B2B or B2C, you’re being weighed against a more sustainable competitor. 175,000+ companies already use EcoVadis (source: EcoVadis, 2026).
  4. Then came the law. Under the EU’s CSRD, the largest companies (1,000+ employees and €450M+ turnover) must report, and they ask their suppliers for the data. Being transparent isn’t enough: you have to show what you’re doing on environment, social and governance.
  5. Starting early is the key, and it isn’t hard once you understand it: measure, decide, act, disclose.
  6. Where do you start? Use the free resources below, or write to me at dakshmehta077@gmail.com.
